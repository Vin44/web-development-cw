Feedback

This page is the Feedback section of the OceanPulse: Life Below Water website. It includes a navigation bar at the top that allows users to access different pages such as Home, Gallery, Action Impact Simulator, Feedback, Our Team, and Site Map. The page also displays several available programs like the Clean Ocean Initiative, Coral Reef Restoration, Sustainable Fishing Practices, and Beach Clean-Up Events, where users can learn more or get involved. Below that, the Ratings and Reviews section shows the overall rating and comments from users who participated in these programs. At the bottom of the page, a feedback form allows visitors to share their opinions and experiences to help improve the programs.

Technologies Used: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
