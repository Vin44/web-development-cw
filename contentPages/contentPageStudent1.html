What are Coral Reefs?

Coral reefs are underwater ecosystems made up of tiny animals called coral polyps. These reefs are often called the “rainforests of the sea” because they support a huge variety of marine life.

Why Coral Reefs Matter

Coral reefs provide shelter and food for millions of marine species. They also protect coastlines from waves and storms and support fishing and tourism industries.

Major Threats to Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are under serious threat due to climate change, ocean pollution, and destructive fishing practices. Rising ocean temperatures cause coral bleaching, which can lead to reef death.

Solutions and Conservation Efforts

Governments and organisations are working to protect reefs through marine protected areas, coral restoration projects, and reducing pollution. Sustainable practices can help reefs recover.

What You Can Do

  • Reduce plastic use and recycle properly
  • Participate in beach cleanups
  • Support eco-friendly products
  • Spread awareness about ocean conservation
